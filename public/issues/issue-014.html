What to watch
This week's AI acquisitions highlight a trend towards vertical integration and platform control. Companies are seeking to own more of the AI stack, from foundational models to data management and specialized applications. Expect continued consolidation as major players aim to capture market share by offering end-to-end AI solutions, and startups focus on niche capabilities that complement these larger ecosystems. Data classification and specialized AI tooling remain key acquisition targets.
