What to watch
This week's activity shows a clear trend of established companies acquiring specialized AI talent and infrastructure to bolster their existing offerings. From travel planning to legal tech, and crucially, to the foundational compute power needed for AI development, acquisitions are driven by a desire to integrate advanced capabilities. Expect this pattern to continue as more sectors recognize the strategic imperative of AI integration and seek to acquire rather than build.
