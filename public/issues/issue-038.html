What to watch
This week highlights a significant consolidation in AI infrastructure, with major tech players like Palo Alto Networks and Nvidia investing billions to secure compute and model development capabilities. The sheer volume of acquisitions, from chip manufacturers taking stakes in AI labs to specialized AI firms being integrated into larger platforms, indicates a race to capture market share and talent. Expect continued M&A activity focused on foundational AI technologies and companies that can accelerate development and deployment.
