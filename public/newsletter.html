This week's biggest deal
Nvidia buys AI platform Hugging Face from OpenAI in a $13 billion deal
Reported by Business Standard →

Nvidia's massive $13 billion acquisition of Hugging Face signals a significant strategic shift. By acquiring this leading AI platform, Nvidia is cementing its dominance not just in AI hardware, but also in the crucial AI development and deployment ecosystem. This move allows Nvidia to deepen its integration with developers and offer a more comprehensive end-to-end solution, further entrenching its position in the rapidly expanding AI market. The acquisition underscores the industry's consolidation around key infrastructure providers.

This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: major players are consolidating the AI landscape by acquiring foundational platforms and specialized tooling.
