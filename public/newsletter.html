Nvidia Reportedly Targets Hugging Face Acquisition for $12.9B

This move would give Nvidia unparalleled control over a critical hub for open-source AI models and tools, directly competing with cloud providers and solidifying its dominance in AI infrastructure. The logic is clear: secure a central platform for model development and deployment, further locking in customers and shaping the future of AI accessibility.
