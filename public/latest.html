This week's biggest deal
Stripe snaps up AI gateway startup OpenRouter for over $7 billion
Reported by Adgully.com →

Stripe's massive $7 billion plus acquisition of OpenRouter signals a major strategic shift. The payments giant is moving beyond traditional financial infrastructure to build a comprehensive AI platform. By acquiring an AI gateway, Stripe aims to control the flow of AI model access and integration for its vast customer base. This move anticipates a future where AI is deeply embedded in all business transactions, positioning Stripe as a central player in that ecosystem.
