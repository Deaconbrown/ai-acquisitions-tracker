What to watch
This week's acquisitions underscore a strong trend towards embedding AI capabilities deeper into enterprise software and specialized industry verticals. Companies are prioritizing AI to enhance data intelligence, customer engagement, and operational efficiency. The focus on agentic AI, as seen with Workday, suggests a move towards more autonomous and proactive AI solutions. We will continue to watch for consolidation in areas providing foundational AI services and those applying AI to specific industry challenges.
