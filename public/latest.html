Digital Science Acquires Jenni AI, Bolstering AI Tools for Research and Academia

Digital Science's acquisition of Jenni AI signals a significant move into the academic and research software space. This acquisition integrates an AI-powered writing and research tool with a platform serving millions of students and researchers. The strategic logic is clear: leverage AI to enhance the productivity and output of academic work, bridging the gap between discovery and dissemination. This trend highlights the growing demand for AI-driven tools tailored to specific professional and educational workflows.
