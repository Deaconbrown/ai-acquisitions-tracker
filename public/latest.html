What to watch
This week saw a diverse range of AI acquisitions, from large-scale infrastructure plays like IFS acquiring EmpowerMX to targeted talent and technology grabs like OpenAI's move for Sonder. The recurring theme is the strategic integration of AI capabilities into existing product suites and the acquisition of specialized AI expertise. Companies are looking to enhance their offerings with AI, either by building it themselves or by acquiring companies with proven solutions. We expect to see continued consolidation as more established players seek to leverage AI for competitive advantage and new entrants focus on niche AI applications.
