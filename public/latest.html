What to watch
This week shows a clear pattern of established companies and well-funded startups acquiring specialized AI capabilities. We expect this trend to continue as companies seek to quickly build out their AI stacks rather than develop them internally. Watch for more acquisitions that bring niche AI expertise into broader platforms.
