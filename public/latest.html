What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: the deepening integration of AI into core business functions, from data center power to real estate and accounting. The potential mega-deal for Hugging Face underscores the immense strategic value placed on foundational AI platforms. We will continue to watch for acquisitions that bolster AI capabilities in specialized industries, as well as those that aim to consolidate or dominate the open-source AI ecosystem. The market is clearly signaling a drive towards both specialization and platform consolidation.
