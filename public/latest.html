What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a strong trend towards vertical integration in the AI space. Companies are acquiring specialized tools and platforms to enhance their core offerings, moving beyond generic AI development. Expect to see more deals where established tech players or rapidly growing AI companies bolt on adjacent functionalities to create more comprehensive solutions, particularly in areas like data management, developer tooling, and specialized industry applications.
