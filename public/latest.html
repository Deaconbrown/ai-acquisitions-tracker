Algolia Acquires Velou to Advance AI Product Intelligence Across Every Search, Recommendation and AI Agent
Reported by Business Wire →

Algolia's acquisition of Velou signals a clear trend towards enhancing AI's role in core business functions like search and recommendations. This move underscores the increasing demand for sophisticated AI solutions that can drive personalized user experiences and improve operational efficiency across e-commerce and digital platforms. The strategic logic centers on expanding Algolia's platform value by embedding more advanced AI directly into its search and discovery products.
