The acquisition of Anyscale by Nvidia-backed Nscale for $1.65 billion is a significant indicator of market consolidation and the immense value placed on foundational AI infrastructure. This deal signals a strong trend towards larger players acquiring specialized AI talent and technology to accelerate their platform development.
