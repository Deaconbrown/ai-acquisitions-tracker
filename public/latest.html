What to watch
This week's acquisitions show a clear trend toward expanding AI capabilities, particularly in specialized domains like writing instruction, marketing data, and patient outcomes. We are also seeing strategic moves into compute infrastructure. The Manus funding round, despite geopolitical challenges, highlights the immense value and perceived future potential in the AI sector. Keep an eye on acquisitions that integrate AI into existing business processes and those that build foundational AI infrastructure, as these will likely drive future growth.
