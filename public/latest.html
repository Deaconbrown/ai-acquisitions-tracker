Microchip Technology Completes Acquisition of Hailo
Reported by Microchip Technology →

Microchip Technology's acquisition of Hailo signals a strategic move to bolster its edge AI capabilities. Hailo's specialized AI processors for edge devices are highly sought after as demand grows for on-device intelligence. This deal underscores the increasing importance of specialized hardware in making AI more efficient and accessible at the edge, integrating advanced AI inference directly into embedded systems.
