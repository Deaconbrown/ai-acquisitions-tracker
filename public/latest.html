This week's biggest deal
AI startup Manus raises $500m after Meta acquisition blocked by China
Reported by Yahoo Finance Singapore →

The geopolitical climate is increasingly shaping AI dealmaking. Manus's substantial funding round, following a blocked acquisition by Meta due to Chinese regulatory concerns, highlights the dual forces of rapid innovation and national security interests. This suggests that the AI landscape is becoming bifurcated, with companies needing to navigate both technological advancement and international regulatory environments. The strategic logic for Manus is clear: secure capital to continue independent growth amidst these complex global dynamics.
