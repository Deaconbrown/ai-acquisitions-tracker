Workday Acquires C1.ai, Signaling Focus on Agentic Identity
Reported by Gartner →

Workday's acquisition of C1.ai signals a clear strategic intent to bolster its capabilities in agentic identity management. This move indicates a broader industry trend towards AI-driven automation of complex identity and access workflows. By integrating C1.ai's technology, Workday aims to enhance its platform's intelligence, providing more proactive and adaptive solutions for enterprise identity governance. The focus on independent controls is crucial for maintaining trust and security in these advanced systems.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a continued push for AI integration across diverse industries, from B2B marketing and healthcare to industrial aftermarkets and retirement services. The common thread is the strategic acquisition of specialized AI capabilities to enhance existing platforms or enter new markets. We expect this trend to accelerate, with companies prioritizing AI to gain a competitive edge in areas like data observability, customer experience, and specialized domain expertise. Keep an eye on acquisitions that promise to bring generative AI or advanced machine learning to solve specific business problems.
