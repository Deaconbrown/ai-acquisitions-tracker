For AMD, this acquisition is a clear play to capture market share in the rapidly expanding AI inference sector.

What to watch
This week highlights a clear trend towards companies integrating AI capabilities deeper into their core offerings, particularly in hardware and specialized software. The acquisition of Taalas by AMD, for instance, points to the growing importance of dedicated AI silicon. We are also seeing AI being leveraged to enhance existing industry workflows, from managed IT services to property valuations. Expect continued consolidation as companies seek to embed AI across diverse sectors and enhance specialized hardware for AI workloads.
