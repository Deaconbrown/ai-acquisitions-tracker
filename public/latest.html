OpenAI Acquires Israeli-Founded AI Camera Company For More Than $300 Million
Reported by THEJ.CA →

OpenAI's acquisition of an Israeli AI camera company for over $300 million signals a significant move into specialized AI hardware. This acquisition suggests OpenAI is looking to deepen its control over the entire AI stack, from foundational models to the specialized hardware that powers them. The strategic logic appears to be about integrating advanced camera technology directly into its AI development, potentially for robotics, advanced vision systems, or even to enhance its own large language models with superior visual input.
