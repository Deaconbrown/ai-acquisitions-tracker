Snowflake Launches Cortex AI Gateway to Control AI Agents and Prevent Runaway Enterprise Costs
Reported by VentureBeat →

Snowflake's launch of Cortex AI Gateway marks a pivotal moment in enterprise AI adoption, positioning the data cloud as the central control plane for AI agent activity. This move addresses the critical need for security, governance, and cost management as AI agents become more autonomous. By enabling secure interoperability and providing a unified system of record for agent actions, Snowflake aims to prevent fragmented AI ecosystems and costly operational overruns, signaling a shift towards platform-based AI governance.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: companies are focused on enabling and securing the AI agent ecosystem. From data center management to cybersecurity, and now to the critical governance of AI agents themselves, the market is rapidly evolving. Expect continued consolidation around platforms that offer robust control, trust, and visibility into AI operations. The race is on to build the essential infrastructure that underpins widespread enterprise AI adoption, moving beyond individual AI models to managing their complex interactions.
