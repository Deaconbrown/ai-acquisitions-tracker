Manus Resumes Independent Operations After Meta's $2 Billion AI Acquisition Revoked by Chinese Regulators
Reported by TradingKey →

China's regulatory intervention has forced Meta to abandon its $2 billion acquisition of AI firm Manus. This development marks a significant setback for Meta's expansion plans in the AI space and highlights the increasing geopolitical complexities surrounding cross-border technology deals. The revocation signals a cautious approach by Chinese authorities towards large-scale foreign acquisitions of advanced AI companies, potentially impacting future M&A activity.
