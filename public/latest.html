This week's biggest deal
Schneider's PTC acquisition brings together 'one hell of an industrial stack'
Reported by Fierce Network →

Schneider Electric's bold acquisition of PTC for $22.6 billion signals a significant consolidation in industrial automation. This deal is a clear bet on integrating AI into the core of manufacturing and operations. By combining Schneider's industrial software and hardware with PTC's product lifecycle management and IoT capabilities, the merged entity aims to offer a comprehensive digital transformation suite. This strategic move will likely push competitors to either acquire similar capabilities or face being outmaneuvered in a rapidly evolving market.
