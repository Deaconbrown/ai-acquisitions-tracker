Exclusive: Salesforce Acquires Startup Cresta for $2.7 Billion
Reported by The Information →

Salesforce's substantial $2.7 billion acquisition of Cresta signals a major strategic push into AI-driven customer engagement. This deal underscores the immense value placed on generative AI capabilities that can directly enhance sales and service operations, a core tenet of Salesforce's platform. The acquisition allows Salesforce to integrate advanced AI conversation intelligence into its existing offerings, providing a powerful competitive advantage and driving deeper customer value. It highlights the trend of large enterprise software players consolidating best-in-class AI startups to accelerate their product roadmaps and solidify market leadership.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a strong focus on integrating AI into core business functions, from cybersecurity to sales and procurement. The significant deal by Salesforce signals that large software vendors are actively consolidating AI capabilities to enhance their existing platforms. We will continue to watch for further consolidation as companies seek to embed advanced AI into their product suites to maintain competitive parity and offer enhanced value to customers. Expect more strategic tuck-in acquisitions and larger transformative deals as AI matures.
