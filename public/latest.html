Germany's Infineon acquires Bengaluru-based C2i Semiconductors to strengthen AI data centre power business

Infineon's acquisition of C2i Semiconductors signals a significant investment in the foundational hardware powering AI. As AI workloads intensify, the demand for efficient power management solutions in data centers grows. This move by Infineon, a major semiconductor player, underscores the strategic importance of specialized components for the booming AI infrastructure market. The integration of C2i's expertise will likely bolster Infineon's competitive edge in providing critical power solutions for AI's energy-intensive future.
