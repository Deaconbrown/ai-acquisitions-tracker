This week's biggest deal
Uber launches parking service following SpotHero acquisition, with an eye on robotaxis
Reported by TechCrunch →

Uber's integration of parking services, enabled by its SpotHero acquisition, highlights a significant strategic shift beyond core ride-hailing. This move signals a broader ambition to control more of the urban mobility ecosystem, providing a seamless experience for users. The inclusion of parking is a logical step towards optimizing the infrastructure needed for future autonomous vehicle operations, particularly robotaxis. It demonstrates a clear playbook of acquiring complementary services to build a more comprehensive mobility platform.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions reveal a strong trend toward vertical integration and expanding service portfolios. Companies are actively acquiring capabilities to offer more comprehensive solutions, especially in areas like mobility and data infrastructure. Keep an eye on acquisitions that integrate AI directly into core business operations or enhance essential infrastructure for AI deployment.
