Stripe Acquires OpenRouter in $7B AI Infrastructure Deal
Reported by The Cryptonomist →

Stripe's acquisition of OpenRouter for $7 billion signals a major shift in the payments giant's strategy. This move indicates Stripe's intent to deeply integrate AI infrastructure into its core financial services. The deal highlights the growing need for specialized AI platforms that can handle complex computational demands, a crucial element for scaling advanced AI applications. By bringing OpenRouter in house, Stripe aims to control a critical piece of the AI development pipeline, ensuring efficiency and security for its future offerings.
