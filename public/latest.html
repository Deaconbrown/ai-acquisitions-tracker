What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear drive towards vertical integration and specialization within the AI landscape. Companies are either acquiring niche capabilities to enhance existing platforms or bolstering their infrastructure to support emerging AI applications. The focus on specialized AI, whether for real estate, broadband networks, or agentic initiatives, suggests a maturation of the market beyond broad AI tools. We expect this trend to continue, with further consolidation around companies possessing deep industry knowledge and proprietary data sets.
