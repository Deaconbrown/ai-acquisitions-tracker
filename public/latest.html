What to watch
This week's deals highlight a broad range of AI adoption strategies, from infrastructure build-outs for AI workloads to the integration of AI tools for specific functions like coding and observability. The acquisition of book data by AI firms, while ethically complex, underscores the continued demand for diverse datasets. The increasing focus on AI for core business functions signals a maturing market where AI is becoming less of a novelty and more of a necessity.
