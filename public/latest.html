This week's biggest deal
Shifting to physical AI, Cognex to acquire RealSense in $500 million deal
Reported by The Jerusalem Post →

Cognex's significant $500 million acquisition of Intel's RealSense division signals a major shift towards integrating AI into the physical world. This move prioritizes machine vision capabilities, enabling robots and automated systems to better perceive and interact with their environments. The strategic logic centers on bridging the gap between AI's analytical power and its practical application in manufacturing and logistics, a critical step for broader industrial adoption.
