Stripe acquires OpenRouter for over $8 billion in major AI bet, boosting token optimization for businesses
Reported by ET CIO →

Stripe's reported $8 billion acquisition of OpenRouter marks a significant strategic move into AI infrastructure. This deal signals increasing enterprise focus on optimizing AI model performance and cost efficiency, particularly concerning token usage. By integrating OpenRouter's capabilities, Stripe aims to streamline AI deployments for businesses on its payment and financial infrastructure platform. The acquisition underscores the trend of established tech companies acquiring specialized AI capabilities to deepen their existing ecosystems.
