SpaceX reportedly eyes $60 billion acquisition of AI coding platform Cursor

The potential $60 billion acquisition of AI coding platform Cursor by SpaceX signals a significant strategic move by Elon Musk's aerospace giant. This deal suggests a growing recognition within large, non-traditional tech companies that AI development tools are critical infrastructure. By acquiring Cursor, SpaceX likely aims to accelerate its own AI initiatives and potentially integrate advanced coding assistance across its vast engineering operations. The sheer valuation underscores the perceived strategic importance of specialized AI tooling.
