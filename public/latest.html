Databricks Acquires Electric to Enhance AI Agent Database Capabilities
Reported by The New Stack →

Databricks' acquisition of Electric signals a significant trend in the AI infrastructure space. By integrating Electric's PostgreSQL database capabilities, Databricks aims to provide each AI agent with a dedicated, robust data backend. This move addresses the growing need for specialized data management solutions as AI agents become more sophisticated and independent. It suggests a future where granular data control and isolation are paramount for agent performance and security, moving beyond generic data lakes.
