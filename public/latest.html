What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: companies are aggressively acquiring AI capabilities across various domains. From data and infrastructure to specialized tooling and core hardware, the race to integrate AI is intensifying. We are seeing a continued focus on companies that can either provide essential AI infrastructure or offer specialized AI applications for specific industries. Expect this consolidation to continue as major players seek to secure their AI advantage through strategic M&A.
