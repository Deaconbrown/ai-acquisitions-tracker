What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a broad range of AI integration strategies. Companies are acquiring AI capabilities for everything from edge computing and building automation to data platforms and AI model distribution. Expect continued consolidation as larger firms seek to embed AI across their existing product lines and infrastructure. The focus will likely remain on companies that can provide tangible efficiency gains or unlock new revenue streams through AI.
