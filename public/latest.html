Stripe Acquires OpenRouter for $7B+, Turning Model Routing Into a Payments Infrastructure Problem
Reported by forkast.news →

Stripe's $7 billion acquisition of OpenRouter signals a significant shift in how AI infrastructure is being built. By bringing model routing into its payments ecosystem, Stripe is positioning itself as a central player in the AI stack, essential for managing and monetizing AI model access. This move integrates complex AI operations with financial rails, suggesting a future where AI deployment and payment processing are tightly coupled. It highlights the growing need for robust infrastructure to support the burgeoning AI economy.
