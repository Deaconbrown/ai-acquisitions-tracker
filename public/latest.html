What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: AI is no longer a standalone product category. Companies are integrating AI capabilities into existing platforms to enhance functionality and user experience, whether in creative software, customer experience solutions, or infrastructure management. The acquisition of specialized AI tooling by larger players, as seen with Adobe and Topaz Labs, suggests a maturing market where AI is becoming a core component rather than an add-on. Expect continued consolidation as businesses across sectors seek to embed AI to gain competitive advantages.
