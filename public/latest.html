What to watch
This week saw continued consolidation as larger software players integrate AI capabilities through acquisitions. Nutanix made a notable move by acquiring Ryax Technologies, focusing on agentic AI and GPU utilization, indicating a growing emphasis on specialized AI infrastructure. The trend suggests companies are prioritizing specific AI functionalities to enhance their core offerings rather than building everything from scratch. Expect more tuck-in acquisitions and strategic partnerships aimed at acquiring niche AI expertise and technology as the market matures.
