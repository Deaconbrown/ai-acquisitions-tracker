This week's biggest deal
Nvidia buys AI platform Hugging Face from OpenAI in a $13 billion deal
Reported by Business Standard →

Nvidia's reported $13 billion acquisition of Hugging Face marks a significant consolidation in the AI ecosystem. This move signals a strategic push by Nvidia to control key infrastructure and platforms that underpin generative AI development. By integrating Hugging Face's vast repository of models and tools, Nvidia can further solidify its dominance in AI hardware and software, offering a comprehensive solution to developers and enterprises.
