Meta Acquires Stilla.ai, Targeting Business Agent Commerce
Reported by quasa.io →

Meta's acquisition of Stilla.ai signals a strategic push into agent-based commerce solutions. This move indicates Meta's intent to leverage AI for more personalized and efficient business interactions, moving beyond passive content consumption. The integration of Stilla.ai's technology likely aims to enhance Meta's existing platforms by enabling sophisticated AI agents that can facilitate transactions and customer service. This acquisition underscores the growing trend of major tech players consolidating AI capabilities to build next-generation commercial ecosystems.
