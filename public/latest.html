What to watch
This week's AI acquisitions highlight a clear trend toward deep vertical integration and the pursuit of proprietary data advantages. From financial data powering AI models to specialized hardware and AI security, companies are aggressively consolidating to control key pieces of the AI value chain. Expect continued M&A activity focused on acquiring niche AI technologies and the data sets that fuel them, particularly as larger players seek to de-risk and accelerate their AI strategies in an increasingly competitive market.
