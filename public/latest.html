AI-music startup Grai acquires social-music service Hangout

Grai's acquisition of Hangout highlights a growing trend of AI companies expanding beyond core technology into adjacent user experience platforms. This move signals a strategic push to integrate AI capabilities into broader social ecosystems, aiming to capture more user data and engagement. The logic is clear: by owning both the AI engine and the social interface, Grai can accelerate product development and create a more cohesive user experience, potentially unlocking new monetization avenues.
