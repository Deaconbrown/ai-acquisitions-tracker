What to watch
This week saw a diverse range of AI acquisitions, from enterprise software enhancements to strategic healthcare plays and expansion in European markets. The notable acquisition of Counsel Health by Oura for $625 million highlights the premium placed on AI technology that can derive actionable insights from complex health data. As AI continues to mature, expect more strategic integrations as companies across sectors seek to leverage AI for deeper customer understanding and market expansion. The focus will likely remain on AI that solves specific industry problems, rather than broad, unproven platforms.
