DOJ questions Nvidia's $12.9 billion Hugging Face acquisition
Reported by Pasquale Pillitteri →

The Department of Justice's scrutiny of Nvidia's planned acquisition of Hugging Face signals a heightened focus on AI market concentration. Regulators are examining how such deals could stifle innovation and limit competition in a rapidly evolving sector. This move suggests that even large, foundational AI acquisitions may face significant antitrust headwinds, impacting future consolidation strategies for major players.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a dual trend: large tech giants like Apple are quietly snapping up AI capabilities, while regulatory bodies are beginning to scrutinize the competitive landscape. The DOJ's attention to Nvidia's proposed Hugging Face deal underscores the increasing importance of AI infrastructure and talent in defining market power. We will continue to watch for more targeted acquisitions by major players and an increasing regulatory lens on these foundational AI asset purchases.
