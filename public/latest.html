What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: AI is no longer a standalone category but a foundational technology augmenting existing business functions. Companies are integrating AI to enhance talent deployment, streamline logistics, and improve customer service in established industries. The focus is shifting from pure AI innovation to AI's practical application in solving real-world business challenges, suggesting future deals will prioritize demonstrable ROI and market integration.
