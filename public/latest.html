What to watch
This week's acquisitions reveal a clear pattern of AI companies seeking to deepen their integration into specific industry verticals and enterprise workflows. From Oura's health focus to OpenAI's e-commerce push and Avanade's automation strategy, the trend is toward AI becoming a specialized tool rather than a generalized platform. Expect continued consolidation as larger tech players acquire specialized AI capabilities to enhance their existing product suites and expand their market reach.
