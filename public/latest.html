What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: large corporations are aggressively integrating AI into their core businesses. From agriculture giants like John Deere to tech behemoths like Amazon, the drive to acquire specialized AI talent and technology is palpable. We are seeing significant capital deployed not just for foundational AI models but also for applications that enhance specific industry verticals like supply chain intelligence and crop analytics. Expect this consolidation to continue as companies race to build comprehensive AI ecosystems and secure a competitive edge.
