Meta Acquires Sweden's Stilla.ai to Advance Business Agents Across WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram
Reported by citybiz →

Meta's acquisition of Stilla.ai signals a strategic push to integrate advanced AI capabilities into its messaging platforms. By acquiring Stilla.ai, Meta aims to enhance business agents, allowing for more sophisticated automated customer interactions across WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram. This move reflects Meta's ongoing strategy to monetize its vast user base through integrated business solutions and to compete in the rapidly evolving landscape of conversational AI.
