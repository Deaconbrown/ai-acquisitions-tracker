This week's biggest deal
SpaceX Closes $60bn Acquisition of AI Coding Agent Cursor
Reported by verdict.co.uk →

SpaceX's massive $60 billion acquisition of Cursor signals a strategic pivot towards integrating advanced AI capabilities directly into its core operations. This move underscores the growing understanding that AI, particularly in code generation and assistance, is becoming a foundational element for complex engineering and development. By bringing Cursor in house, SpaceX is likely aiming to accelerate its own product development cycles and optimize its vast engineering efforts through custom AI tools. This acquisition highlights the intense competition for AI talent and technology, as established players recognize the need to own and control critical AI infrastructure.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a dual trend: massive, strategic acquisitions of core AI technology by industry giants, and the ongoing consolidation within the AI infrastructure layer. The sheer scale of the SpaceX and Stripe deals indicates that companies are prioritizing direct control over critical AI capabilities. We will continue to see large incumbents snapping up promising AI startups to accelerate their internal roadmaps, while specialized infrastructure players face pressure to scale or be acquired. Expect more moves in model routing, optimization, and AI compute as companies race to build out their next-generation platforms.
