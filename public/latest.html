What to watch
This week's acquisitions underscore a clear trend: companies are aggressively integrating AI capabilities to enhance existing platforms and services. From employee performance and legal workflows to defense systems and government technology, the pursuit of AI is broad and deep. Expect to see continued consolidation as companies seek to acquire specialized AI talent and technology, particularly in areas promising significant efficiency gains or new competitive advantages. The focus remains on practical application rather than pure research.
