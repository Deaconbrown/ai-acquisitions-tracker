What to watch
This week's AI acquisitions show a clear pattern of established companies integrating specialized AI capabilities to enhance existing products and services. From smart home technology to fitness software and even defense applications, the trend is towards acquiring AI expertise rather than building it in-house. We should expect this to continue, with companies across various sectors looking to bolster their AI offerings through strategic acquisitions. Keep an eye on companies in more traditional industries exploring AI integration, as they will likely be the next wave of acquirers.
