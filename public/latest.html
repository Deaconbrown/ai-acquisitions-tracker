What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a robust appetite for AI across diverse sectors, from senior care and real estate to automotive and semiconductor industries. The recurring theme is the integration of AI capabilities to enhance existing platforms and unlock new efficiencies. Expect continued consolidation as companies seek to acquire specialized AI talent and technology, particularly in data infrastructure and specialized applications. The increasing focus on AI's power consumption, as seen with Infineon, suggests hardware and infrastructure companies will remain key acquisition targets.
