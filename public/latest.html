This week's deal activity highlights a persistent trend: AI's integration into core business functions, particularly cybersecurity and specialized infrastructure. Investors are betting on AI's ability to provide defensible advantages and efficiency gains across diverse sectors, suggesting a continued focus on AI-native solutions and companies that can demonstrate clear ROI in these expanding fields.
