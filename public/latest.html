This week's biggest deal
Five9 Acquires Level AI For Standalone Contact Center AI
Reported by CMSWire →

Five9's acquisition of Level AI signals a strong push into specialized AI solutions within enterprise software. The deal highlights the increasing demand for AI capabilities that can be integrated into existing customer engagement platforms, enhancing specific functions like contact center operations. This move is strategically sound, allowing Five9 to offer a more robust and AI-native solution, potentially capturing a larger market share in the competitive customer experience technology space. It underscores a trend where established players are acquiring niche AI expertise to accelerate their product roadmaps.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: companies are not just buying AI technology, but also AI-enabled solutions and the infrastructure to support them. From contact center AI to digital engineering and data center capacity, the focus is on integrating AI into core business functions. Expect continued consolidation as larger firms seek to quickly embed AI capabilities, either through direct acquisition of specialized AI companies or by acquiring infrastructure that enables their own AI development and deployment.
