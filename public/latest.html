What to watch
This week's AI acquisitions show a clear trend. Companies are not just buying AI technology, but rather teams and integrated solutions. Focus areas include customer intelligence, patient engagement, and mainframe modernization, all seeking to embed AI into core business functions. We expect this pattern to continue, with acquirers prioritizing targets that offer immediate value and strategic alignment over purely research-driven ventures. Look for more niche acquisitions that bolster specific industry verticals.
