This week's biggest deal
Stripe acquires OpenRouter for over $8 billion in major AI bet, boosting token optimization for businesses
Reported by ET CIO →

Stripe's reported acquisition of OpenRouter for over $8 billion signals a significant shift in how businesses will interact with large language models. This move suggests that sophisticated routing and optimization of AI models is becoming a critical business function. By integrating OpenRouter, Stripe is positioning itself to manage the complex web of AI model calls, likely focusing on cost efficiency and performance for its vast merchant base. This acquisition underscores the escalating value of AI infrastructure and the strategic importance of controlling access to and the performance of these powerful tools.

This week's acquisitions highlight a maturing AI ecosystem, with significant investment flowing into core infrastructure and specialized tools.
