What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: established companies are actively seeking to integrate AI to enhance existing products and services, rather than just acquiring AI specialists for their technology. From learning platforms and customer service software to e-commerce and home security, AI is being woven into the fabric of diverse industries. We anticipate a continued surge in these strategic integrations, as companies across sectors recognize the imperative to leverage AI for competitive advantage and operational efficiency. Expect more deals focused on augmenting rather than replacing core business functions.
