Marvell Bets on AI Data-Centre Growth With $115 Billion Lumentum Acquisition
Reported by CXO Digitalpulse →

Marvell's massive acquisition of Lumentum signals a clear strategic play for the burgeoning AI infrastructure market. By combining their strengths, Marvell aims to establish a dominant position in optical interconnects, a critical component for the high-speed data transfer required by AI workloads. This deal underscores the massive investment flowing into the foundational elements of AI, moving beyond software and models to the hardware that powers them.
