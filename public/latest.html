What to watch
This week saw a clear trend of established companies integrating AI into specialized sectors, from biomanufacturing and aviation to insurance and enterprise transformation. The acquisition of AI companies by non-AI native firms highlights the broad applicability of AI across industries. We will continue to watch for similar strategic integrations, especially in areas like advanced manufacturing and specialized enterprise software, as companies seek to leverage AI for competitive advantage. The ongoing consolidation suggests that AI capabilities are becoming a foundational element for growth and innovation across the economy.
