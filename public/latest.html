It represents a strategic move to safeguard its chip business by controlling a key platform for AI model deployment and further integrate into the software and services layer of the AI ecosystem.

What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a clear trend: AI is no longer just about foundational models or hardware, but about embedding intelligence into specific industry applications and integrating complex AI platforms. From agriculture to automotive repair, companies are seeking to leverage AI for specialized functions. The potential Nvidia-Hugging Face deal underscores the strategic importance of AI platforms and open-source communities. We expect continued consolidation as established players acquire specialized AI capabilities to enhance their existing offerings and enter new markets.
