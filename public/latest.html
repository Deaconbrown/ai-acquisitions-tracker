Amazon Nears $7.5 Billion Acquisition of AI Startup Decar
Reported by GuruFocus →

Amazon's reported $7.5 billion pursuit of AI startup Decar signals a significant move into the competitive AI chip market. This acquisition, if finalized, would directly challenge Nvidia's dominance. The strategic logic points to Amazon building its own AI silicon capabilities, reducing reliance on external suppliers and customizing hardware for its vast cloud infrastructure. This move underscores the intense race among tech giants to control foundational AI technology.
