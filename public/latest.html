Digital Science Acquires Jenni AI, Linking It to Dimensions Database
Reported by Unite.AI →

Digital Science's acquisition of Jenni AI signals a strategic move to integrate AI-powered writing assistance into its broader research ecosystem. By connecting Jenni AI to its Dimensions database, Digital Science aims to enhance research workflows and provide valuable insights for academics. This acquisition highlights the growing trend of knowledge and research platforms seeking to leverage AI for productivity and discovery.
