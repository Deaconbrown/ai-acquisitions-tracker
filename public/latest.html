Anthropic's reported decision to walk away from a potential $6 billion acquisition of Decart AI signals a new level of scrutiny in high-value AI deals. While the exact reasons remain undisclosed, such moves often indicate concerns uncovered during due diligence, potentially related to technology integration, market fit, or valuation. This signals a maturing AI market where substantial capital is being deployed, but not without careful consideration of the underlying business fundamentals.

What to watch
This week's deal flow shows a split between strategic acquisitions aimed at enhancing core AI capabilities and significant potential deals that falter. While companies like Flex and Stripe are actively integrating AI technologies through M&A, the Anthropic and Decart situation highlights the increasing caution surrounding massive valuations. Investors should watch for continued consolidation in AI infrastructure and tooling, alongside a heightened focus on rigorous due diligence for megadeals.
