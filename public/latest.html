Midjourney's acquisition of Co-Star marks a significant strategic shift for the AI image generation leader. What to watch
This week's acquisitions show a clear trend of AI companies moving beyond core technology to build integrated products and capture more of the value chain. From image generation to coding and building automation, the focus is shifting towards application and tangible use cases. We are also seeing strategic investments in hardware and infrastructure to support this expansion. Expect continued consolidation as established players and emerging leaders seek to broaden their offerings and solidify market positions through acquiring specialized talent and technology.
