What to watch
This week's acquisitions demonstrate a clear pattern of large companies integrating specialized AI capabilities into their existing platforms and expanding into new verticals like meeting intelligence and AI security. Expect continued consolidation as companies seek to leverage AI for market differentiation and enhanced product offerings, particularly in data analytics, automation, and cybersecurity.
