This week's biggest deal
AMD Acquires AI 'Godmother' Fei-Fei Li's Startup for $8.2 Billion
Reported by CNBC →

AMD's $8.2 billion acquisition of World Labs signals a major strategic shift for the chip giant. By bringing in Fei-Fei Li's deep expertise in world models, AMD aims to bolster its AI systems capabilities beyond just hardware. This move underscores the increasing importance of foundational AI research and development in the semiconductor arms race, suggesting that future market leadership will depend on both silicon and the intelligent systems built upon it.
