What to watch
This week's acquisitions highlight a growing trend of companies integrating AI into core operations, particularly in cybersecurity and infrastructure. The substantial hardware acquisition by OpenAI underscores the compute demands of large model development. As AI becomes more embedded, expect continued consolidation as companies seek to acquire specialized AI talent and technology to maintain a competitive edge. The focus remains on building out foundational capabilities and operationalizing AI effectively.
